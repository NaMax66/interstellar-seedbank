## Current English draft

Saturday night. We go on with our lives: making plans, worrying about work, arguing, falling in love, and trying to live up to the roles we've been given.

And somewhere in the cold darkness, an asteroid may be moving toward Earth.

Perhaps it will pass us by. Perhaps we will never even hear about it. Or perhaps, one day, it will make our plans, titles, borders, and social roles suddenly irrelevant.

A human being is fragile in space. So is the civilization we often mistake for something permanent.

We cannot prepare for everything. But we can invest in early detection, planetary defense, science, and emergency planning. We can store water, food, knowledge, and energy.

And perhaps the most important resources are trust, solidarity, and the ability to help one another.

Whatever comes out of the darkness, may we meet it prepared - and remain human.
